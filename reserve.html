What happens next
  1. The service creates a pending reservation reference.
  2. Your wallet sends the exact native ETH amount directly to the displayed treasury address.
  3. The backend checks sender, recipient, amount, successful receipt, and confirmations against its configured chain RPC.
  4. Only the backend-verified transaction is marked paid in the admin ledger.
